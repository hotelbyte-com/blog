Hotel price normalization is risky because suppliers expose different combinations of room price, total price, tax, fees, currency, board, cancellation, and package identifiers.

This whitepaper explains why HotelByte keeps amount and currency semantics tied to their source and avoids borrowing supplier cost, seller amount, customer amount, or profit from each other.

Read this asset if your team needs to evaluate pricing correctness and financial safety across multi-supplier search results.
