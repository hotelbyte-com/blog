Financial surfaces in hotel distribution cannot rely on convenient fallbacks. Amount, currency, supplier cost, tenant seller amount, customer buyer amount, and profit each have separate meanings.

This whitepaper explains how HotelByte protects wallet and credit operations with explicit money semantics and fail-closed behavior for incomplete financial data.

Read this asset if your review needs to inspect booking debit, refund, credit exposure, and financial display correctness.
