Real-time hotel search has to coordinate supplier fan-out, content matching, rate normalization, availability, timeout budgets, and partial failures.

This whitepaper explains how HotelByte makes search aggregation observable so teams can answer both what was returned and why a supplier or hotel did not appear.

Read this asset if you are evaluating supplier aggregation under latency pressure and need evidence beyond simple response counts.
