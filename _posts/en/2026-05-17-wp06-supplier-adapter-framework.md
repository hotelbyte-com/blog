Supplier integration is not just field mapping. Each supplier brings its own search semantics, room/rate identifiers, credentials, session behavior, booking states, and error categories.

This whitepaper explains how HotelByte standardizes those differences behind supplier adapters while preserving supplier-specific boundaries where they matter.

Read this asset if your team needs to evaluate how a hotel API platform absorbs supplier complexity without flattening important contractual differences.
